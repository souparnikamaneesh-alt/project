Kerala Hydration & Water Quality Protocol

Traditional boiled herbal waters (Vellam) promote digestion and prevent water retention

Morning Ritual (6:30 - 7:00 AM)

Jeera Vellam (Cumin water): 1 warm glass on an empty stomach. Awakens digestive fire (Agni) and reduces bloating.

Through the Day

Pathimukham (Sappan wood water) or Lukewarm water: Naturally antibacterial, cools core body temperature, aids kidney clearance.

Post-Meal Rules

Avoid ice-cold water during meals. Sip lukewarm water 30 minutes after eating to facilitate gastric digestion.

Mid-afternoon Hydration

Sambharam (Kerala Spiced Buttermilk): Whisked with crushed green chilli, curry leaves, ginger & pinch of rock salt.

Kerala Fat-Loss Golden Cooking Rules

Simple tweaks that keep genuine Kerala flavors while trimming 400+ hidden calories

🥥

Measure Coconut Oil

1 tablespoon of coconut oil has ~120 kcal. Use a measuring spoon: cap your daily cooking oil to 2–3 teaspoons total.

🍚

Portion the Matta Rice

Limit cooked red rice to 1 medium cup (120g). Double the vegetables (Thoran / Aviyal) to fill up your plate safely.

🍲

Kudampuli Gravies over Coconut Gravies

Choose Meen Mulakittathu or Kudampuli curries instead of heavy coconut milk theeyal or rich kurma.

🍵

Smart Kerala Snacks

Swap fried banana chips and parippuvada with boiled cherupayar, roasted peanuts (small fist), or steamed nendran.
